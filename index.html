How it works
  • Five words. The first is given — you write the next four.
  • Three letters are marked in blue. Your next word must contain all three, in any position.
  • It must also bring in at least one letter the previous word didn't have — no anagram farming.
  • Carried letters came from the word above. Fresh letters have never appeared anywhere in the chain — those are the ones that score.
  • Find 8 letters. Each word can bring in at most 2 the chain has never seen. Every puzzle can be maxed, so 8 of 8 means the same thing every day.
  • Points: 10 for one fresh letter, 30 for two — doubling up is worth triple. Plus each fresh letter's Scrabble value, so Z and Q pay.
  • Play a word within 15s for +10, within 40s for +5. The clock restarts every word, so one long think won't sink the run.
